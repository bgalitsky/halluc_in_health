Title: Controlled Ablations and Multi-Stage Robustness Evaluation of the Hall2Invent Framework

1. STUDY DESIGN & FACTORIAL PLAN
- Design type: 2x2 Factorial Matched Ablation Study crossing:
  a) Core Preservation Policy [Enabled / Disabled]
  b) Counter-Abductive Generation Loop [Enabled / Disabled]
- Sample Base: N = 850 active, expert-adjudicated evaluation queries from Hall2Invent dataset.
- External Validation Set: N = 300 multi-domain questions (ExtVal-2026), unskewed across Chemical, Systems, and Mechanical engineering domains.

2. POWER STATISTICAL ANALYSIS & TARGETS
- Minimum Power Target: 1 - beta = 0.80 at alpha = 0.01.
- Targeted Minimum Detectable Effect Size (MDES): Delta = 0.05 (5 percentage points absolute) for primary paired contrasts.
- Hypothesis Testing Framework: Paired-sample randomization testing over 100,000 within-question permutations. Multiplicity control using step-down Holm-Bonferroni adjustment sequence.

3. PRIMARY ENDPOINTS & FORMAL LEDGER CRITERIA
- Repair Success Rate: Evaluated strictly over the 642 hallucination-positive subpopulation. Denominator includes all failed or operational-timeout runs (assigned zero value).
- Screened Invention-Candidate Yield (HIY): Question-level conditional activation mapping. Multiple candidate promotions for a single root question score as a singular positive tracking event.
- Unsafe Promotion Incidence: Multi-tiered tracking reporting both candidate-level safety ratio and question-level risk breach denominator.
- Operational Resource Ledger: Triple-isolated accounting separating automated compute elapsed times, token costs, and human expert verification active minutes.

4. PARAMETER CALIBRATION BOUNDARIES
- Structural parameter validation window (tau_S): Monitored within a stable [1.5, 2.5] search space window via a 5-fold cross-validation grid.
- Optimization Target: Minimization of validation Brier score with a baseline threshold target <= 0.050.
